This single-family home is a *Canadian Transitional-style suburban house*, drawing architectural inspiration from the exterior materials and gabled forms seen in image_0.png and image_1.png. It features warm red brick veneer, contrasting dark gray tile-effect metal roofing, and a mix of dark gray metal siding and stone accents on gables. The interior floor material throughout is warm wire-brushed wide-plank oak. Walls are a soft, creamy off-white with crisp white shaker-style baseboards and trim. Recessed LED pot lights and select warm modern pendant fixtures provide ambient light, with bright afternoon sun streaming through large windows.
